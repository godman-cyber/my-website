Refund Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2025

1. Refund Eligibility

As our products and services are delivered digitally, all sales are generally final. However, refunds may be granted in the following cases:

  • You were charged more than once by mistake.
  • A technical issue on our side prevented you from accessing the purchased service.
  • Refunds required by applicable consumer protection laws.

2. How to Request a Refund

Please email support@securenetcheck.net with your order details (transaction ID, purchase date, and the email used at checkout). We’ll review your request and reply within 5 business days.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Misuse or misunderstanding of the product features.
  • Issues caused by third-party services not under our control.

4. Subscription & Cancellation

All subscriptions include a 7-day free trial. After the trial, billing renews monthly until canceled. You can cancel at any time via the Paddle receipt/portal or from our cancel page. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

5. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback, we may suspend access to the Service while the claim is reviewed. To avoid delays, please contact us first so we can help resolve any issue quickly.
