Refund Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-20

1. Refund Eligibility

Because our products and services are delivered digitally, all sales are generally considered final. However, refunds may be granted in the following circumstances:

  • You were accidentally charged more than once.
  • A technical issue on our side prevented you from accessing the purchased service.
  • Refunds required by applicable consumer protection laws.

2. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please contact us at the email below and include your order details (transaction ID, purchase date, and the email address used at checkout). We will review your request and reply within 5 business days.

support@securenetcheck.net

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued in cases such as:

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Misuse or misunderstanding of the product features.
  • Issues caused by third-party services not under our control.

4. Subscription & Cancellation

All subscriptions include a 7-day free trial. After the trial, billing renews monthly until canceled. You can cancel at any time via the confirmation email or your account management link. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

5. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback, we may suspend access to the service while we review the claim. To avoid delays, please contact us first so we can help resolve any issue quickly.

6. Contact

Questions about our refund policy? Reach us at support@securenetcheck.net.
