Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-20

1. Information We Collect

  • Network and browser data used to generate diagnostics (e.g., IP, ASN, latency).

  • Billing information is processed by Paddle; we do not store full card details.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve the diagnostic features.
  • Prevent abuse and maintain security.
  • Handle subscription and support requests.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data with service providers only as necessary (e.g., Paddle for payments).

4. Data Retention

Diagnostic results shown in the dashboard are generated client‑side. Your local history is stored on your device and you may clear it at any time. Server‑side logs, if any, are retained for a limited time for security/audit.

5. Your Rights

You may contact us to access or delete information we hold about you where applicable by law.
